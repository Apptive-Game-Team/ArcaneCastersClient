The attached images are references. The first is the game's master rendering style. The second is the approved game title logo on one long wooden plank sign. Copy that plank sign — its hand-cut papercraft look, its irregular chunky facets at the two ends, its colours, its dark brown underside and its upper-left light — but leave out the gold letters and remove the arc.

Fill the entire background with one flat, uniform, fully opaque pure magenta, RGB 255 0 255, hex #FF00FF, edge to edge. The background must be that exact single colour and nothing else: no grey-and-white checkerboard pattern, no white, no grey, no gradient, no texture, no vignette, no ground plane, no cast shadow, no glow or halo bleeding into it. Magenta appears nowhere on the subject itself. Use a square canvas, 1024 x 1024 pixels.

Subject: ONE single empty wooden plank sign for a game menu button, seen from the front and very slightly from above, like the logo sign. It is straight and level — no arc, no bend, no tilt — and exactly 4 times as wide as it is tall: about 960 pixels wide by 240 pixels tall, centered across the middle of the canvas, touching within 30 pixels of the left and right canvas edges, with magenta above and below.

Construction, which matters more than anything else:
- The front face is one wide flat plane of light wood #D9A972 that runs the whole length. Along its top edge runs a thin lighter bevel strip; along its bottom edge runs a thin darker bevel strip in #C08C56, and under that the board's underside and thickness in dark wood brown #5D4032, clearly visible, like the underside of the logo sign.
- These strips are long horizontal bands with straight, parallel, horizontal edges from one end to the other. The long middle two thirds of the plank has nothing else on it: no knots, no nails, no cracks, no notches, no grooves, no grain lines, no split into two planks, no one-off facet. It must look the same if stretched sideways to any length.
- All of the character is at the two ends, within the outer 12 percent of the width on each side. Each end is hand-cut and chunky: the top and bottom corners are clipped off at a slant, and the end is built from three or four irregular faceted planes in #C08C56, #A2703F and #5D4032, lit from the upper left, like the ends of the logo sign. The ends are not identical copies but have the same size and weight.
- The overall outline stays a simple long rectangle with clipped corners: no notch cut into the ends, no forked or split ends.

Rendering technique: faceted low-poly papercraft, flat polygonal planes, one flat colour per plane, hard creases between planes, no texture, no wood grain, no noise, no gradients, no gloss, no specular highlight, no outline stroke. It must not look like a smooth rendered 3D bar or a bevel filter; it must look hand-built from cut paper like the logo sign and the stone tower in the first reference. Restrained saturation.

Exclude: no text, no letters, no icons, no symbols, no rope, no nails, no metal, no frame, no second plank, no stacked planks, no ground, no drop shadow onto the background, no watermark. A single empty plank only.
