Material and colour for this candidate: the letters are carved from the same blue crystal as the gem on the apprentice mage's staff in the reference. Letter faces are faceted sky-blue crystal (#98DEEB light plane, #53B2EB mid plane, #2D92E4 dark plane); the letter sides that show the depth are warm lightning gold (#F6DB5F light, #D7A313 mid, #996B07 dark), like a gold setting. The apprentice mage's wooden staff, with its blue crystal tip, lies diagonally behind the two lines, from lower left to upper right, partly hidden by the letters.
