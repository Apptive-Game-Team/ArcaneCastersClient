# Logo D Wide Prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: approved game title logo layout variant
Input images: `logo-d.png` is the identity, letterform, material, palette, lighting, and sign reference; `MasterStyleKey.png` is the project-wide flat faceted rendering reference.
Primary request: Redraw the approved ARCANE CASTERS logo with the two words arranged on one single wide line. Change only the layout.
Subject: Exact text `ARCANE CASTERS`, with every letter present once and in that order, mounted on one continuous long wooden plank sign.
Style and medium: Hand-built 2.5D cut-paper cartoon made from large flat polygonal planes. Preserve the original chunky custom letter shapes and irregular handmade facets. It must not resemble a rendered three-dimensional model or a standard font with effects.
Composition: Square 1024 by 1024 canvas. Center the logo across the middle at about 92 percent of canvas width. One line only. The continuous plank is about five times wider than tall and follows a very gentle upward arc. Leave broad magenta space above and below.
Lighting: One soft light source from the upper left. Broad upper-left light planes and lower-right shadow planes only.
Color palette: Letter faces use exactly `#F6DB5F` light, `#D7A313` mid, `#996B07` dark. Letter sides use dark warm wood brown `#5D4032`. Wooden plank uses exactly `#D9A972` light, `#C08C56` mid, `#A2703F` dark. Background is flat pure magenta `#FF00FF` covering the full canvas.
Text (verbatim): `ARCANE CASTERS`
Constraints: Preserve the approved logo's letter identity, chunky proportions, planar facet construction, warm wood sign, depth relationship, and upper-left illumination. All letters must remain legible at thumbnail size. One continuous sign, one line, one gentle arc. Background is fully opaque magenta, not transparent.
Avoid: second line, stacked words, missing letters, repeated letters, alternate spelling, subtitle, any other words, watermark, texture, grain, gradients inside planes, gloss, glow, outline stroke, bevel-filter appearance, photorealism, realistic three-dimensional rendering, checkerboard, transparency, scenery, shadows outside the logo.
