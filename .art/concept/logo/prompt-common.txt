Background: fill the entire canvas with flat pure magenta, RGB 255 0 255, hex #FF00FF. No gradient, no texture, no vignette, no shadow on the background. Do not draw a checkerboard. Do not make it transparent.

Canvas: square, 1024 x 1024. The logo is centered with generous magenta margin on every side, filling about 80 percent of the width.

Subject: a game title logotype that reads exactly, in capital letters, on two lines:
line 1: ARCANE  (A - R - C - A - N - E, six letters)
line 2: CASTERS (C - A - S - T - E - R - S, seven letters)
Spell it exactly like that. No other words, no subtitle, no tagline, no numbers, no signature, no watermark.

Rendering technique, taken from the attached reference images, which are the game's master style: faceted low-poly papercraft. Every letter is a chunky solid object built from flat polygonal planes, one flat colour per plane, with hard creases between planes, like the stone tower and the golem in the reference. Light comes from the upper left: upper-left planes are light, lower-right planes are dark, three value bands per material. Thick, rounded, friendly letter shapes with visible depth, seen slightly from above. The two lines sit on a gentle upward arc.

Exclusions: no texture, no grain, no noise, no brush strokes, no gradients inside a plane, no gloss, no bevel highlights, no glow, no lens flare, no outline stroke around the logo, no drop shadow on the background. If it could be mistaken for a rendered 3D model, a digital painting, or a font with a filter on it, it is wrong.
