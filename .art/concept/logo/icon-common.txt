Canvas: square, 1024 x 1024. Fill the entire background with flat grass green, RGB 152 184 82, hex #98B852, edge to edge. No border, no frame, no rounded corners, no vignette, no gradient in the background.

Rendering technique, taken from the attached reference images, which are the game's master style: faceted low-poly papercraft. Light from the upper left: upper-left planes light, lower-right planes dark, three value bands per material. Chunky, cute proportions. Eyes, where there are eyes, are simple dark ovals with one tiny highlight, exactly like the reference characters.

Exclusions: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark. No texture, no grain, no noise, no brush strokes, no gradients inside a plane, no gloss, no glow, no outline stroke. If it could be mistaken for a rendered 3D model or a digital painting, it is wrong.
