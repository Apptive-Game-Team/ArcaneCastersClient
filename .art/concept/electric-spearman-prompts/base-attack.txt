Production sprite proposal for ArcaneCasters, Electric Spearman (전기 창병).
Reference: .art/anchors/master-v2/MasterStyleKey.png, rendering technique only.
Single twin-panel sheet: idle on left, thrust attack on right, same character, scale, camera, ground line and body position. Transparent background, no ground shadow, no text or framing. Each panel contains one full character facing right at three-quarter view. Chunky small friendly World Tree lightning spirit, rounded yellow body, two simple dark oval eyes, small legs ending in soft wisps. It has no armor, manufactured equipment, wood, metal or clothing. A connected broad lightning-shaped extension from its hand/body forms a short glowing spear, pointed forward in attack. The spear is its own lightning, not a carried tool. Compact silhouette, modest expression. Large overlapping matte cut-paper facets, three value bands, broad upper-left light, no outline, no grain or painted texture, no scattered sparks or separate aura. Restrained yellow palette #FCFBD5 #F6DB5F #D7A313 #996B07. No neon bloom. Body fills each panel height and the spear stays inside its panel. Designed to read at 64px, final small-unit sheet frames at most 128x128. Attack pose changes only front arm/spear and slight lean, feet remain planted in the same place.
