Build the ground in three layers, so the surface is richly described rather than plain:

2. **Angular grass detail.** Over the base, scatter many small clusters of sharp, narrow triangular slivers — angular grass blades, three to seven slivers per clump, each sliver a thin straight-sided triangle about one eightieth of the canvas long, fanning out at different angles. They are drawn a step lighter or a step darker than the facet they sit on, so they read as texture catching the light rather than as objects.
