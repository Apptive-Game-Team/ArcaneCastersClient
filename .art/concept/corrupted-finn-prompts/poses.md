# 타락한 핀 자세 생성 프롬프트

내장 `image_gen`을 사용했다. 첫 시트에서 기본과 지팡이를 든 자세를 사용했고, 공격 자세는 같은 손으로 지팡이를 쥐도록 다시 생성했다.

## 기본과 지팡이 들기

참조는 `ApprenticeMage.png`, 기본 외형의 `idle.png`, 만화의 `mage-corruption.png` 순이다.

Create a single game animation sprite sheet, 3 equal-width columns, one row, wide landscape, with the same character repeated in 3 separate full-body poses. Reference 1 is the ArcaneCasters 2.5D low-poly cut-paper render style and body proportions; reference 2 sets runtime camera and size grammar (face right in 3/4 view, with chibi body and large oval eyes). Reference 3 is identity only: adapt ONLY the rightmost stage-three corrupted Finn, maintaining short tousled brown hair, tiny side braid with ivory bead, visible human face, red oval eyes, round basalt clasp with orange cracks, tattered charcoal-and-crimson cloak, ivory under-robe, ochre card satchel, brown boots, and a charred branch staff enclosing a red-orange faceted crystal. Angular black basalt on shoulder and forearm with ember fissures. Keep the same figure silhouette scale, hair, eyes and feet locations in all 3 cells. Left cell IDLE: staff planted with crystal roughly at cheek height, staff visibly to the left of body. Middle cell RAISED: same body, raise staff upright with crystal above hair, elbow lifts. Right cell ATTACKING: same body and foot planting, extend staff horizontally at shoulder level toward screen right, crystal and shaft entirely contained within cell. Right-facing in all 3 cells. Distinct poses. Every part has a safe margin from the cell edges, especially the cast staff. Approximate body height 75% of sheet height. Identical camera and soft top-left light, clean large polygon facets, no ink outline, painterly gradients or anime lines. Eyes simple red ovals, no drawn eyebrows or mouth. Exactly one human mage in each cell. Omit the small golem companion, detached shards, spell VFX, fire trails, aura, shadows, scene, objects, markings, text, dividers, panel borders. Export transparent RGBA with a clean alpha cutout; if unable to output true alpha, use flat pure #FF00FF background with no magenta on the subject.

Create ONLY the corrected third/attack pose for the character Finn on the first sprite sheet reference. Keep the exact existing head, proportions, face, hairstyle, bead, same clothes, round clasp, boots, body height and 3/4 right-facing camera. Use second reference as the exact arm positioning reference, no costume or character changes from that reference. Critical continuity correction: the mage thrusts the staff with the SAME arm as the left and middle poses in the sheet. In the first two poses it is the human skin hand with IVORY SLEEVE attached to the SHOULDER ON THE VIEWER'S LEFT, with the rocky arm on the viewer's right. The dark rocky arm with glowing cracks hangs at the viewer's RIGHT and LOWER down beside the hip below the staff, not on the viewer's left and never holding the staff. Exactly two arms and two hands. Full sprite in frame with margin around entire silhouette, no floating fragments, no effects, no shadows, clean true transparent RGBA. Wide image.
