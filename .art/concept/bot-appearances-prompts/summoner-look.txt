Look "Summoner" (nature and lightning caller, concepts Minion Master and Summoner): a deep forest-green hooded cloak with the hood UP over the head but cut open so the face and both eyes are fully visible, a muted dusty violet robe with a dark green sash (use a muted, greyish blue-violet like #6B6A9E, not a bright pinkish purple), several small hanging spirit charms (tiny pale yellow-green teardrop talismans on short cords) dangling from the hood edge and the belt, a small flat circular summoning-circle glyph as an emblem on the chest, a small satchel at the hip. The staff is dark green-brown wood topped with a large open ring (a circle) with a small pale yellow-green gem floating inside it. Dominant colours: deep green, dusty blue-violet, pale yellow-green. Do not use any pink or magenta tone anywhere.
