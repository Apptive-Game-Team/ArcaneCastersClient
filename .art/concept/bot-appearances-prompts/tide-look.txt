Look "Tide Bomber" (water and wind bomb thrower, concept Water Bomb Maniac): a wide-brimmed blue rain hat (a hat with a broad flat brim and a low crown, saturated blue with a lighter teal band; dark hair visible under it in angular planes), a teal short rain cape, a pale blue robe, a belt with three round glass bubble-bomb flasks hanging from it (translucent light-blue spheres with a white highlight plane and a small cork on top), a small satchel at the hip. The staff is pale driftwood and its head is a splash shape: a big stylised water splash made of five or six curved blue and teal pointed planes fanning upward. Dominant colours: blue, teal, pale blue. No water streams drawn around the character, no droplets scattered around.
Width limit, important: the hat brim is no wider than the hair beneath it plus a little, the teal cape stays short and close to the body (ends at the hip, no train), and the splash staff head is compact: no wider than the head of the character and made of planes fanning upward, not sideways.
